# Brew Guide

A guide that curates manual-brewing recipes from well-known coffee professionals and walks the user through a live brew on a timer.

## Language

### Recipes

**Recipe**:
The full set of brewing parameters and ordered Steps for one way of brewing coffee.
_Avoid_: Method, technique, brew guide

**Built-in recipe**:
A Recipe that ships with the app, attributed to an Author and read-only.
_Avoid_: Default recipe, preset

**Personal recipe**:
A Recipe the user created or copied from another Recipe, which the user can edit.
_Avoid_: Custom recipe, user recipe

**Recipe template**:
The pre-filled starting shape the editor opens with when the user creates a new Recipe.
_Avoid_: Blank recipe, scaffold

**Recipe family**:
A group of Recipes that share an Author and a technique, such as the variants of the 4:6.
_Avoid_: Series, collection

**Author**:
The professional a Recipe is attributed to; for a Personal recipe, the user.
_Avoid_: Creator, brewer (see Brewer), chef

**Library**:
The single screen where all Recipes are browsed, searched, filtered and favorited.
_Avoid_: Dashboard, home

### Brewing

**Brewer**:
The dripper or vessel a Recipe is brewed with, such as a V60 or an Aeropress.
_Avoid_: Device, dripper, equipment

**Step**:
One ordered instruction in a Recipe, with a kind and a start time measured from the beginning of the brew.
_Avoid_: Stage, phase, instruction

**Pour**:
A Step that adds water to the coffee, either a bloom or a regular pour.
_Avoid_: Addition

**Grind setting**:
The 1Zpresso ZP6 click number a Recipe calls for, either an estimate for a Built-in recipe or the user's own value.
_Avoid_: Grind size, grind level

**Session**:
One live run-through of a Recipe on the timer, working from a snapshot of the Recipe taken at the start.
_Avoid_: Brew, run, timer
